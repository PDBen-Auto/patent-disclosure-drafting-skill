# Synthetic Example: Modular Desk Lamp Housing

This is a fully synthetic teaching case. It is not copied from an actual product, patent, applicant, supplier, or filing strategy.

## Brief

**Working title:** Modular desk lamp housing with replaceable light module

**Route:** combined appearance disclosure and utility-model/utility-patent technical disclosure

**Jurisdiction:** unspecified; local agent review required

**Problem:** A small desk lamp needs a replaceable light module, stable tilt adjustment, and a clean exterior that can be manufactured with two shell parts.

**Synthetic constraints:** keep a compact footprint, allow tool-free module replacement, and avoid loose external fasteners on the visible front surface.

## Technical disclosure outline

The lower shell 10 defines a receiving region for the light-module insert 20. The tilt bracket 30 is coupled to the lower shell 10 and supports the insert 20 in at least two angular positions. The rear latch 40 releasably retains the insert 20 so a user can replace the module without removing the visible front shell surface.

The agent should confirm whether the pivot, latch, and receiving region are essential relationships or merely one embodiment. Alternative latch locations and bracket geometries should be listed as optional embodiments only after technical support is supplied.

## Appearance disclosure outline

The proposed design is the overall visual appearance of the assembled desk lamp housing as shown in the complete view set. The draft should focus on the shell silhouette, the recessed front transition, the visual relationship between the insert and lower shell, and the visible tilt bracket treatment. Functional performance and hidden latch mechanics belong in the technical route, not the appearance description.
