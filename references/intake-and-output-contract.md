## Quality labels

Use exactly one primary label per statement:

- `observed`: directly visible or present in a supplied record;
- `inventor-provided`: stated by the user or inventor but not independently checked;
- `inferred`: reasoned from evidence and clearly marked;
- `proposed wording`: drafting suggestion for review;
- `unknown`: not supplied or not verifiable.
